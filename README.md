One line of text, roasting a smart fridge that refuses to open after midnight — entered in the [Midnight Fridge Roast](https://make.fun/bounty/u-carbon-forge-midnight-fridge-roast-fc5d44a7) bounty on make.fun.

## The line

> At midnight the fridge beeped, locked, and showed a photo of me at 2 a.m. last Tuesday holding the ice-cream tub like a trophy.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`). The house judge compares entries two at a time; the funniest roast of a smart fridge that refuses to open after midnight wins.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
